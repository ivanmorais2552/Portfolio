The folder contains 108 files: 98 PNG exports and 10 design-system PDFs. The PNGs are generally high-quality exports, with full screens at 3,840px wide and supporting crops between roughly 300px and 1,600px wide. The screenshot data is fictional and does not expose personal or confidential information, per Ivan's confirmation. ## Recommended case-study direction

Use `admin/Home Dashboard.png` as the hero candidate. It is a full product overview at 3,840 × 2,718 and visually communicates submissions, presenters, RSVP, printing, judging, and administrative oversight in one screen. It shows aggregate data rather than obvious direct identifiers, but it should still receive a final privacy review before publication.

Use full-screen exports as large editorial visuals and use modal, filter, tab, and dropdown crops as supporting visuals beside process explanations. Keep the case study centered on the actual workflow: submissions → printing → judging → awards, with administration as the oversight layer.

## Proposed visual layout

1. **Hero / overview** — Case-study title, role, scope, and a large framed `admin/Home Dashboard.png`. Use a restrained reveal animation and a caption stating that the image is an original Fulton design export.
2. **Context and responsibility** — Short narrative about the Mendix-to-Angular transition, faculty workflow frustrations, stakeholder collaboration, and the role of biweekly reviews. No unsupported outcomes.
3. **The workflow map** — Use `wireframes/Fulton Judging Wireframe.png` as the process anchor, paired with a compact four-step map: Submissions → Printing → Judging → Awards. Administration appears as the oversight layer.
4. **Designing the system** — Show the strongest design-system excerpts or a carefully selected static crop from the PDFs after confirming the intended excerpt. Keep the PDFs out of the initial page load.
5. **Submissions** — Use a redacted version of `submissions/Submission.png` or `Submission-1.png` large, with `Add Co-Presenters.png` and `Add Mentors.png` as supporting crops.
6. **Printing** — Use `printing/Printing@2x.png` large with `Print Modal@2x.png` and the larger print-status filter as supporting details. Exclude `Manage Printers@2x.png` until email addresses are removed.
7. **Judging** — Use `judging/Assign Judges (Lead Judge).png` and `judging/Scoring.png` as the main pair. The existing interactive role-switching demo can use these as the visual references, while fictional interface state remains clearly labeled as a recreation.
8. **Awards** — Use a redacted `awards/Awards Manager.png` or `Form.png`; exclude `awards/Review.png` because of tax and signature content.
9. **Reflection / closing** — Summarize the iterative, workflow-by-workflow process and link back to the work overview. Keep the conclusion focused on the design decisions and responsibilities supported by the source material.

## Privacy gate before implementation

The current folder is confirmed safe from a personal-data perspective because all screenshot content is fictional. Before publishing, review the following screens for presentation context and whether the subject matter belongs in the case study:

- W-9, direct-deposit, tax, and signature content in `awards/Review.png` may distract from the design narrative even though it is fictional.
- Email fields in `printing/Manage Printers@2x.png` may be visually dense at portfolio scale.
- Names, Net IDs, poster filenames, research abstracts, and uploaded content in `submissions/Submission.png` and `admin/Admin Submission Details.png` may be better used as supporting visuals than hero images.
- Payment, RSVP, dietary-information, and other operational screens should be selected based on narrative value.

The recommended implementation can use the original exports as provided, preserve the files untouched, and include a visible note that the visuals are original Fulton design exports where applicable.
